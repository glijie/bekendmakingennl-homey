Follow official Dutch public notices around an address, town, village, district, neighbourhood or municipality. Add a location to Homey, set a radius for an address if needed and use Flows to receive alerts for newly found publications.

Read recent notices in the app settings, including the full titles, dates, authorities and links to the original publications. Bekendmakingen NL uses public KOOP and PDOK data and is an independent app, not a government service. Notices with missing or inaccurate source location information may not be found or may be marked as uncertain.
